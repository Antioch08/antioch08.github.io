Battlemage

A solo 1v1v1 MOBA total conversion for StarCraft II, from design documents through to final implementation.

Battlemage pits warring magical institutions against one another in a free-for-all battle for control of the land. Level up your faction leader, upgrade your minions or call in powerful reinforcements, then lead the charge to wipe out your opponents' headquarters.

The mod puts a MOBA twist on StarCraft II, totally converting its units, structures, environments and parts of the UI. Players fight for control points at strategic locations and use skill, minions and elite units to overpower their foes.

The goal was to push my production and design skills: a unique, fully playable experience taken from early concepts all the way to technical and artistic implementation, backed by full documentation including flowcharts and behaviour diagrams.

What I did

  • Gameplay and system concepting and design.
  • Pre-visualisation: diagrams, flowcharts and design documentation.
  • Gameplay event and trigger scripting.
  • Re-texturing, modifying, importing and implementing art assets.
  • Ability design and balancing for a 1v1v1 MOBA.
  • Coordinated outsourced artwork (unit portraits).
  • Map design.
  • PC & Modding
  • Economy
  • Level Design
