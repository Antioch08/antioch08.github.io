Play as a chicken in a colourful world of whimsy and mayhem. Peck up grass to lay eggs and nurture them until you have a swarm of small, adorable and unique chicks of your own to look after and protect. Team up with fellow chickens (or don't), gather resources and build the ultimate coop to survive the night, when the big bad wolves come out looking for a meal.

Chicken Life is a popular Roblox roleplaying game that reached the top 100 charts and top 100 benchmarks for D1 retention. As Design Director I owned its roadmap, building a programme of features and improvements from KPI data to make it the greatest chicken game on Roblox.

What I did

  • Product owner: coordinated the roadmap and feature development, targeting improvements at specific KPIs.
  • Designed features and systems, plus targeted improvements to the core gameplay.
  • Oversaw the core gameplay overhaul of eggs and hatching, which introduced unique, collectable chicks and a progression system. Player conversion rose by 0.6% and D1–D7 retention by 6%.
  • Designed and oversaw best-practice improvements to the in-game shop and monetisable power-ups, which tripled player spend.
  • Introduced new cosmetics for player models, eggs and building pieces as part of ongoing LiveOps delivery.
