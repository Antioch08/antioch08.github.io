Rick & Morty: Clone Rumble

A squad RPG on a huge licensed IP: quests, metagame balancing, gacha economy and LiveOps through soft launch.

Studio
Big Pixel Studios
Platform
Mobile (iOS & Android) · Unreleased
Role
Game Designer
When
Feb 2019 – Nov 2020

Rick & Morty: Clone Rumble is a collection RPG for the hugely popular Rick & Morty IP, from the creators of Pocket Mortys. Players collect and upgrade a roster of quirky clones from the show and battle rival Ricks to become the Rickest Rick of them all at Rickmania, the hot new interdimensional contest.

I designed the questing system and worked with the creative director and outsourced writers on on-brand dialogue and narrative for every adventure, leading a pod of developers and artists through new content.

During soft launch I took over metagame balancing: the economy of upgrade items and feature rewards, gacha drop rates, and monetisation features such as bundles and special offers.

What I did

  • Designed and implemented exploration and mission content.
  • Iterated on content using internal and external playtests and live player data.
  • Built the content creation pipeline.
  • Oversaw a team of artists and developers on mission content, assets and features.
  • Economy balancing: drop tables, resources and gacha balance.
  • Balanced sales, offers and reward calendars.
  • Used analytics to make targeted improvements during soft launch.
  • LiveOps design: scheduled events and sales and chose experiments for A/B tests.
