About

I'm a game design leader who loves creating delightful, engaging and surprising experiences for players around the world. Over more than a decade, from QA through to Design Director, I've worked across almost every part of game design: free-to-play mobile and match-3, Roblox games and LiveOps, AAA modding, scripting and worldbuilding.

Today I'm a Senior Game Designer at Madbox in Barcelona, working on Pocket Champs with a focus on metagame, economy, LiveOps and monetisation.

My interest in games started early with map editors and modding tools for The Elder Scrolls: Oblivion and Skyrim, Warcraft III and StarCraft II, and that's where I learned design, content creation and scripting. Outside of games I'm into new technology, architecture, psychology and investing.

  • Over a decade of industry experience and design leadership in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Managed, mentored and led teams of designers and creatives.
  • Shipped multiple titles from ideation through development, then ran their live operations and events.
  • Design direction, product ownership, roadmapping and working with stakeholders and executives.
  • Designing to KPI targets and folding data insights and player feedback back into the design.
  • Spreadsheet wizard with economy design and modelling experience.
  • Strong writing, mission and quest design, and scripting experience.
  • Clear design communication with 2D/3D visualisation tools, Unity and Unreal Engine.
