Mystery Match, Crafty Candy and Pirate Puzzle Blast are a suite of casual match-3 flagship titles from mobile studio Outplay Entertainment. I was Lead Game Designer on Pirate Puzzle Blast, overseeing every area of the game from concept through to live operations, and it won the TIGA 2018 award for Best Puzzle Game.

I worked with executives, stakeholders and the team at every level to design features that raised the quality of the game and hit KPI targets and business objectives. I managed the design team's performance and professional development, and kept events and a steady cadence of new content flowing through the game's live operations.

I supported the team through organisation, strategy and collaboration to improve our core KPIs: retention, engagement and monetisation.

  • Worked directly with executives and stakeholders to steer product design.

  • Took several titles from concept development through to delivery.
  • Used data analysis, models and tools to understand player behaviour and improve KPIs.
  • Co-designed and balanced complex game systems.
