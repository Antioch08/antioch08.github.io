Enrol in an educational institute like no other. The Multiversity studies the knowledge of all worlds and keeps the most valuable artifacts of the known and unknown cosmos. Meet the faculty and your fellow students, sit your exams, and venture into bizarre and bedazzling worlds to retrieve artifacts for the archives.

The Multiversity is a four-chapter set of experiences built for The Sandbox. Players explore the campus and take portals to outlandish worlds, completing quests, battling local nuisances and rival treasure hunters, solving puzzles and dodging hazards along the way.

What I did

  • Worked with a small internal team of artists to conceptualise and build the world from start to finish.
  • Owned all technical design and implementation.
  • World and level design with The Sandbox's voxel tools, from greybox to polished final environment.
  • Built quest logic with node-, trigger- and event-based setups.
  • Ran playtests and qualitative feedback sessions.
  • Worked directly with The Sandbox team to deliver milestones smoothly.
