Pocket Champs is Madbox's live idle racing game on iOS and Android. Players train and upgrade their Champs and gadgets, climb the Trophy Road through leagues and islands, and race rivals around the world, with new seasons, events and offers rolling out every few weeks.

My role covers three core responsibilities: system and feature design, economy balance, and monetisation (MTX). In practice that means designing late-game progression and new currencies, rebalancing the game's economy, and owning the offers, seasons and webstore programmes that sit on top of them.

My biggest win was the Trophy Road rework, which removed the churn spike at the Champions League wall and lifted late-game spend and long-term retention. While daily players dipped, revenue per player and daily payer conversion both reached their highest levels in August 2026.

What I did

  • Trophy Road rework: lead designer. Designed the new format of the Champions League and introduced Island Cups as a new endgame progression system, including its race structure and reward systems, to keep endgame players engaged. Endgame churn fell 53%, IAP ARPDAU rose 40% for late-game players, and D90–180 retention improved 8%.
  • Island Choice & Fortunes: led design of Trophy Road phase 2, a new late-game spend loop. Champions League daily payers rose 17%, and the new island offer converted at over twice the rate of the one it replaced.
  • Wild Cards: took a new upgrade currency from idea to A/B test to full release. Wild Cards now fund about 30% of gadget upgrades (up from 11%) and became the #2 special-offer line in their first month.
  • Gadget Rebalance: built an AI-assisted economy simulator and used it to rebuild shard and gold distribution across the gadget economy. The rebalance delivered longer-term depth of spend by refocusing spending on upgrading gadgets rather than first obtaining them, and shipped to all players.
  • MTX ownership: offer optimisations (Intro Power Offer +22% purchases per DAU, Progress Pack +66% revenue per DAU), new Progression Packs that drove 4.7% of all first purchases in their first month, season offers across 8 LiveOps seasons, and an offer-quality analysis that now steers the offer roadmap.
  • Webstore loyalty programme: designed the monthly loyalty ladder, which helped grow webstore revenue roughly 3× in four months alongside Direct to Checkout.
  • Training Playground: owned the design of the stickiest daily event at launch, with 66% daily engagement and up to 10% more playtime.
  • Design lead on major game releases, managing the smooth implementation of other team members' design work into each release version. Also rebuilt the team's GDD process and mentored junior designers.
