Train Sim World: Tycoon combines a streamlined train simulator with the 'build and expand' tycoon genre on Roblox. Players are newly appointed Station Masters, building and upgrading their ever-expanding station to earn money for more trains. Its huge hand-crafted world can be explored as a driver or a passenger, and it was built to inspire a whole new generation of young train fans.

I was the sole designer, taking the game from initial concept to finished design and owning feature production and iteration throughout, working closely with Dovetail Games.

The game drew more than 2k concurrent players at launch, plenty of praise from popular YouTubers and a place in the Roblox top 300 by active players. That beat Dovetail's own commercial releases of Train Sim World 3 and 4, making it the most popular train game in the world at the time.

What I did

  • Designed the initial features that took the game from concept to prototype and on to full release.
  • Set up the content pipeline and the initial content requirements.
  • Owned the tycoon economy balance and pricing.
  • Designed and configured the in-game shop and MTX products.
  • Worked directly with environment and station artists so unlocks flowed cohesively with the tycoon progression.
  • Troubleshot technical elements such as track layout.
